Recipe: Super Awesome Salad. Served with our house sauce.
Allergens: egg, sesame, shellfish
Flavour: smoky with a hint of citrus

Ingredients:
One head of lettuce, washed and chopped
Parmesan cheese, grated
Grilled chicken, in pieces
Croutons
House sauce

Method:
In a big bowl, mix the lettuce and chicken.
Pour the house sauce and mix well to coat the salad.
Top with cheese and croutons.
